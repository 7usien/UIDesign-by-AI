# Category playbooks

Use a category to identify proof and visitor decisions, never to select a template.

- **B2B software:** show a real product state, an operational outcome, and one low-friction next action; reject decorative dashboards.
- **Professional services:** show tangible deliverables, method artifacts, and honest scope; reject invented client logos or generic service tiles.
- **Hospitality and property:** let verified place, atmosphere, availability, and action carry the page; reject motion that obscures booking or location facts.
- **Legal and regulated services:** prioritize comprehension, jurisdiction, and careful claims; reject urgency effects that weaken credibility.

Every category must pass the substitution test: replacing the nouns with an unrelated offer must break the composition, proof format, or signature mechanism.
