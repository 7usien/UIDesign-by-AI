# External Skill Research

This agent synthesizes selected mechanisms from current open agent-skill work while keeping one coherent landing-page workflow.

## Adopted mechanisms

- [Anthropic frontend-design](https://skills.sh/anthropics/skills/frontend-design): ground art direction in the subject, make the hero a thesis, use structural devices only when they encode meaning, and take a justified aesthetic risk.
- [Taste Skill](https://skills.sh/leonxlnx/taste-skill/design-taste-frontend): infer the brief before styling, control variance/motion/density deliberately, scan for recurring AI-template patterns, and run a concrete preflight check.
- [Elaya landing-page-design](https://skills.sh/elayadesign/ai-design-skills/landing-page-design): one offer, one priority audience, one primary action; preserve message scent; place proof and objections where they affect the decision.
- [Impeccable](https://skills.sh/pbakaus/impeccable/impeccable): treat the first render as a draft and use a bounded desktop/mobile inspect-correct-confirm cycle.
- [Vercel web-design-guidelines](https://skills.sh/vercel-labs/agent-skills/web-design-guidelines): separate correctness and accessibility review from subjective visual taste.

## Deliberately excluded

- universal font, palette, radius, framework, or component-library prescriptions;
- mandatory gradients, glass navigation, word-by-word reveals, or animation recipes;
- fixed section counts and a mandatory FAQ, logo wall, tagline block, bento grid, or hero formula;
- forced imagery when typography, product behavior, data, or another medium better serves the concept;
- unbounded interview sequences or autonomous polish loops.

These exclusions protect transferability: the brief and evidence choose the form.
